# 🛍️ FirstCry Sales & Product Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Power BI Sales & Product Analytics Dashboard** created using FirstCry sales data.

The dashboard is designed to analyze sales performance, customer activity, product performance, brand performance, sales channels, order status, and state-wise sales performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Track orders, customers, quantity, and average order value
- Identify top-performing products and brands
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Compare sales across different channels
- Analyze category and state-wise performance
- Understand order status distribution
- Provide interactive filters for business analysis

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel / CSV
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Sales Overview

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value

**Key Visuals:**
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Channel
- Sales by Category
- Top States by Sales
- Order Status

**Filters:**
- Order Date
- State
- Category
- Sales Channel

### 2. Product & Brand Analysis

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Products
- Total Brands
- Total Sub-Categories
- Average Unit Price

**Key Visuals:**
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Top 10 Brands by Sales
- Quantity by Brand
- Average Unit Price by Brand
- Product Performance Table

**Filters:**
- Brand
- Category

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- Monthly sales patterns and trends
- Top-performing products and brands
- High-performing states
- Sales contribution by channel
- Order status distribution
- Product quantity and pricing patterns
